# Glossary

Vocabulary for Mr. Robot. Specs, code and tests use these words and no synonyms.

- **Space** — one deployment's group of people who may share secrets, subscriptions, skills, definitions and robots: a household or a company. v1 deploys one Space. Its admin is the Space admin; its shared memory file is SPACE.md. (Called Home until v1.18.)
- **Member** — a person signed in through Cloudflare Access who belongs to a Space. The first Member is the Space admin.
- **Robot** — a persistent worker owned by a Member: one Durable Object, one endless Conversation, its own Persona files, Grants and Files, and optionally a Workspace. Its work is kept as Tasks at the Space. Not a chat session; its Subagents are its tools, not Robots.
- **Subagent** — a second agent a Robot starts inside its own Durable Object for one task: a fresh context (subagent) or one that inherits the Robot's (subagent_fork), on a model from the subagents grant's list. It has the Robot's grants minus delegation, Robot messages, Threads, shared-file edits and Asks; its report wakes the Robot. Never a Robot, a Fleet row or a Task's doer. At most three run at once; depth one.
- **Mr. Robot** — the personal chief Robot every Member gets on first sign-in; it may create Robots, holds recipient Grants to every Robot the Member can reach, and creates and changes Tasks as one of them (robot_id). Sits in a side drawer of Mission Control.
- **Conversation** — the single, endless exchange with a Robot: a chain of Chapters, the Live chapter and the ones before it on its chain. Rendered simply as chat (one scroll over every Chapter, stored as chat items) or fully as the Trajectory (one Chapter at a time); both come from the same session log.
- **Chapter** — one span of a Conversation: one DSH session, from a compaction checkpoint (or a Rewind's point) to the next. It starts with a seed (what the model saw when it opened), has its ordinal, the Chapter it came from, its dates, its Turns, and how it closed: by a compaction or by a Rewind. A Subagent belongs to the Chapter it ran in.
- **Live chapter** — the Chapter the Robot works in: the only one a Turn resumes and writes, at most a context budget long.
- **Superseded chapter** — a Chapter a Rewind went back before: kept, readable and greyed in the Trajectory, off the Conversation; the Robot does not see it and it cannot be rewound to.
- **Cold chapter** — a closed Chapter outside the retention window (the last 30 Chapters or 60 days, whichever keeps more), whose events are one object in R2; its chat items and search stay, its Trajectory is fetched when opened. Never deleted by age.
- **Trajectory** — the full log view of one Chapter: Turns with their Wake-up and Task, tool calls, results, executed code; Previous, Next and a list of the Chapters (dates, Turns, how each closed).
- **Turn** — one agent-loop run of a Robot, from a Wake-up to the Robot going back to sleep. One Turn at a time per Robot; steers reach it at its next step, follow-ups wait for Turns of their own. The Robot's Subagents may still run after it ends.
- **Wake-up** — what starts a Turn or reaches a running one: a Member message, a Task whose moment came or that a person ran now, a Robot message, a Channel event, a Takeover returned, a Subagent's report, or a platform notice. Each carries a delivery, Steer or Follow-up, decided from its sender. The Turn records the Wake-up that started it as its cause.
- **Steer** — the delivery of a Wake-up from the Robot's owner (in the app or on a Channel that identifies them) or from the owner's Mr. Robot directing his staff: it reaches a running Turn at its next step, and starts a Turn when none runs. The Robot says so when it changes its work, and changes its Task focus with a reason.
- **Follow-up** — the delivery of every other Wake-up (Tasks, webhooks, other Robots, Threads, platform notices, and the owner's Ctrl+Enter): it waits for a Turn of its own. Follow-ups from one sender queued together share one Turn; a Task never shares one.
- **Task** — something to do: a title, instructions, who created it, its assignee (the doer: a Member, a Robot, or nobody; the code and tools say assignee), an optional parent Task, a Timing, and a state of open, done or cancelled. Never "in progress" or "failed": working is a fact about a Turn, failing a fact about an Attempt. Kept by the Space. Only task_done makes a Robot's Task done.
- **Timing** — when a Task wakes its doer: *unscheduled* (on the list, an optional deadline), *scheduled* (a moment, an optional deadline not before it) or *recurring* (a rule, a time zone and the next occurrence; no deadline; only a recurring Task can be paused).
- **Attempt** — one Turn of a Robot on a Task: started, finished, outcome (running, ok, failed, skipped, interrupted) and the first line of the reply. A Task keeps its last 20. Overdue is derived: a scheduled Task whose moment passed without an Attempt.
- **Mission Control** — a Member's page with two dense tables: the Fleet (each Robot he reaches: state, what woke it, the Task it works on, the running tool, since when) and the Tasks he sees, grouped by doer, parent or time. The Space admin's Whole Space switch widens both to every Robot and Task.
- **Rewind** — opening a new Live chapter seeded with what the model saw at a chosen point, in the Live chapter or an older one; every Chapter after the point becomes a Superseded chapter. No note enters the context: the next Wake-up's label says that external effects since the point still stand. There is no undo.
- **Grant** — permission a Member gives a Robot: a tool, a skill, a recipient Robot, a secret. Everything is off until granted.
- **Grant proposal** — a Robot's request for additional Grants, stored exactly and answered by the owner; the stored proposal is applied, never prose.
- **Files** — the Robot's files in R2: Persona files, memory, notes, attachments, artefacts, Pages. The Place named `files`, for files only; it runs no command. (Called Workspace until v1.21; for one release `on: "workspace"` from a Robot without a Workspace still reaches its Files, with a notice.)
- **Workspace** — a Host and a root directory on it where a Robot works, as in DeepSeek Harness: a Robot setting (none by default), checked on the Host and kept with the repository root above it (the nearest .git ancestor). The Place named `workspace`: that Host, with paths relative to the Robot's current directory there, which working_directory moves within the repository and the platform keeps; a Subagent has its own, starting in the Robot's. Several Robots may share one root. The Box is not a Workspace (its /workspace is scratch).
- **Place** — where a Robot's tool acts, and the word every call must name as `on`: the *files*, the *workspace* (when the Robot has one), the *box*, or a Host by its name (two Hosts the Robot reaches with one name go by their owners' names too, "Desk (Anna)"). Nothing is a default, and a call to a Place the Robot holds no Grant on is refused. The files group opens the Files, the box grant the Box, and a Host's files and shell facets that Host, and the Workspace on it.
- **Persona files** — SOUL.md (who the Robot is, how it talks, what it never does: the portable description) and AGENTS.md (how it works here: conventions, lessons, and a Tool notes section), with MEMORY.md (three sections: Facts, Preferences, Commitments) and the daily notes memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md; owned by the Robot.
- **File history** — the kept versions of a persona, memory or Member file and of SPACE.md: each with who wrote it (the Robot, a person, Mr. Robot, the Memory sweep, a restore) and when; the last 50 per file, at most 90 days old, in R2 outside the Files. A restore is a new version.
- **Memory sweep** (Sweep) — the bounded step of an Idle compaction after the Snapshot, on the Robot's own model and warm cache, run only when a daily note changed since the last one: it moves what lasts from the daily notes into MEMORY.md's sections, corrects what is superseded where it stands and invents nothing. Its writes are versions by the sweep; the Trajectory records what it changed; its cost is memory's.
- **Snapshot** — the bounded memory step before every compaction, in a Turn or idle: three model steps with the file tools on the Files only, in which the Robot writes to its daily note what is in flight and to MEMORY.md what lasts. Skipped when nothing happened in the chapter since the last snapshot or sweep. Its writes are versions by the snapshot; its cost is memory's.
- **Daily notes** — memory/YYYY-MM-DD.md, the raw detail of a day. They are not in the context: the Robot finds them with Memory search and reads today's with read.
- **Memory search** — the tool memory_search over the Robot's MEMORY.md, AGENTS.md, SOUL.md, daily notes, dreams, its owner's files (People pages included) and SPACE.md: ranked paragraphs with path, lines, date and snippet, from a full-text index in the Robot's Durable Object that every write updates. Full text only: no vectors, no stemming.
- **People page** — memory/people/<name>.md, one page per person a Member deals with, at the Member scope: every Robot of the Member writes it directly (the one Member file not proposed) and the file history names the Robot; INDEX.md is the platform's. Only the Member and, when merging two pages of one person, Mr. Robot delete one.
- **Dream** — once a night at 03:00 in the owner's time zone, for a Robot with dreaming on and something to look back at: a Turn with the cause dream, on the dream model, at most 12 steps and no tool that acts on the world, that writes dreams/YYYY-MM-DD.md, rewrites AGENTS.md's "How to act for <owner>" (30 lines at most), proposes skills or Member-file edits and may leave one question for the morning. Mr. Robot's dream also reads his owner's Robots' notes and tends the People pages; no memory moves between Robots. Its cost is memory's.
- **Duty** — something a Robot does between Turns, registered by the plugin that owns it on the alarm (warming, the idle compaction, the dream-hour close, the dream, the Box idle stop).
- **Platform step** — one bounded instruction the platform gives the Robot's own agent outside a person's Wake-up, with a step limit, a tool allow-list, an author and a usage source: the safety net, the Snapshot, the Sweep, the definition scrub.
- **Definition** — a Robot as files, to install for someone else: SOUL.md, AGENTS.md, MEMORY.md without owner data, skills/, README.md (title, paragraph, what the interview asks), and tasks.yaml and grants.yaml, which the platform writes and reads (YAML 1.2, no unknown keys) and people only read; connectors named by kind and logins by site, never a Connection or an entry. A snapshot: nothing links it to the Robot it came from. It lives in the Space's library, from robots/<slug>/ in the library repository or saved from a Robot; Download definition gives its directory to commit by hand.
- **Marketplace** — the page of the Space's Definitions: title, paragraph, needs (marked when the Member has them), author and date, with Add, which makes a Robot of his in setup from it. The Space admin publishes and removes; a Grok Bot template is imported as a brief, labelled an imported summary.
- **Member files** — USER.md and PROACTIVE_PREFERENCES.md, owned by a Member, mounted read-only into each of their Robots.
- **Takeover** — a Member taking control of a Robot's browser tab from their device (screen, taps, keys) and handing it back; the Robot's Turn is suspended meanwhile.
- **Live view** — watching a Robot's browser without taking it over.
- **Channel** — an adapter that turns external events into Wake-ups and Robot output into outbound messages on the same Conversation. v1: PWA and Web Push.
- **Provider** — a model source: OpenAI subscription, Anthropic subscription, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Workers AI. Credentials belong to a Member and may be shared with the Space.
- **Spend limit** — a monthly cost cap per Robot and per Member, counting every source (model, browser, Box, services); crossing it stops the Robot's Subagents, then blocks the Robot until raised.
- **Auto review** — a Robot setting: before each call of an item at allow, a reviewer model (the Robot's choice, else the Space's) classifies the call low, medium or high and allows it or denies it; a denied call becomes an Ask with the reviewer's reason, a failed review fails the call. Off by default.
- **Cache warming** — a Robot setting: while the Robot is idle, its last request is sent again for one output token at 54, 108 and 162 minutes on a one-hour cache, so the next message reads a warm cache; then the Idle compaction closes the chapter at 216. On for Mr. Robot by default; only for a Provider with a known cache window.
- **Idle compaction** — a Robot setting: while the Robot is idle, one compaction on the warm cache, after the Snapshot and the Memory sweep: at 54 minutes, or at 216 for a Robot that warms its cache (after the third warming), so an idle chapter closes within four hours; skipped below a floor of context tokens. On for every Robot by default. At the dream hour a chapter still open is closed the same way, whatever its size.
- **Box** — a Cloudflare container a Robot with the box grant runs commands on, built from one image that holds what an agent reaches for (Python with Pandas, Node, Bun, git, ripgrep, ffmpeg and more; no browser). Its disk is scratch except box/, a small folder copied in from the Files at boot (with box/setup.sh run) and back at Turn end (its uv and Bun caches go whole, as box/.cache.tar.zst, when the Box stops); it stops ten minutes after its last command or Job. Its running time is a cost. Not the owner's computer: that is a Host. Never "Computer".
- **Job** — a command that goes on by itself: a run still going at its time limit, or one started with run_in_background, on the Box or on a Host, or a Host command whose connection dropped while the run waited. Jobs of a Robot are one list. It ends completed, killed or lost; when it ends by itself or is lost it wakes the Robot as a Follow-up with its exit code and the last 40 lines, the whole output under spill/jobs/.
- **Job on a Host** — a Job that runs in the Mr. Robot app on a Host. It lives as long as the app runs: it is lost when the app restarts or the Host stays away for more than five minutes, and the app stops it when the Host is unpaired. A reconnect of the same app loses nothing. The Box's Job lives as long as its Box.
- **ws** — the program in the Box that reaches the Robot's Files from a script: ls, get, put and rm. It works only while the Box lives, only on that Robot's files, and only with the box grant and the files group at allow, since a script cannot wait for an Ask. A run of ws that the platform starts (for copy, or for the caches) carries a permit for its one file instead. It is how a big file gets to the Box and back without a stop in between.
- **Spill** — the complete result of a tool call over the inline budget, kept under spill/ in the Files while the model gets its head, its tail and the path; tidied after 30 days.
- **Skill library** — the Space's single collection of skills in R2, synchronised from a Git repository and extended by approved Robot proposals; granted per Robot.
- **Robot message** — a work request or a question from one Robot to a granted recipient Robot, or its reply, delivered through a durable outbox and shown as a labelled message.
- **Code mode** — the default execution mode (DSH PTC): the model writes a program that calls its tools inside an isolate; the alternative is direct tool calls.
- **Browser backend** — what runs a Robot's Chrome behind the browser seam: Browser Run (in the cloud) or a Host browser (on a real computer). Chosen per Robot, defaulting to the Space's.
- **Login entry** — name, username, password, websites, notes, scope; granted per Robot; filled into matching pages without the password reaching the Robot's program. Replaces Secret.
- **Ask** — a pending decision a Robot puts to its owner (setup approval, grant proposal, member-file edit, skill, Takeover, review, Thread invitation, tool call, question, credential), shown in place of the composer. Stored exactly; the stored Ask is applied, never prose.
- **Question** — the Ask kind of the ask tool: one prompt with up to six options (each a label, a value, an optional description and a style: default, primary or danger), one answer or several (multi-select), optionally the owner's own words, optionally dismissed when the owner moves on. The call returns at once; the answer wakes the Robot as a Steer. On Discord the owner answers with the numbers of the options.
- **Draft** — a tool-call Ask of a send tool (Gmail send, reply and forward; Slack and Discord messages), shown as the message it would send: recipients, subject, body in Markdown, attachments. The owner edits it and sends exactly that, or discards it with a reason; the Robot revises it with draft_revise, and the card shows the words that changed. A send tool at ask always makes one; draft: true makes one at allow. Not a draft in the mailbox: gmail_draft leaves one there. "This Robot sends on its own" raises the Connection's write grant to allow.
- **Always allow** — the owner's answer to a tool-call Ask that sets the items the call waited on to allow for that Robot and runs the call; the Conversation records it with the Ask's id. Never offered on an Auto review denial or a call without the sandbox.
- **Credential request** — the Ask kind of request_credential: the Robot names a login or a secret it needs and why; the owner fills the Login entry form, which creates the entry under the owner and grants it to the Robot, or declines. The password never reaches the Robot.
- **Say** — the Robot's one voice to its owner: each say call is one message in the Conversation and on the Channel the Wake-up came from, with optional attachments (files of its Files, screenshots). Text written without say stays in the Trajectory. A Turn of the owner that ends silent gets a platform step and, at last, a fallback say of its last text.
- **Chief question** — a robot_send with question set to true, to the Robot's chief (its owner's Mr. Robot, for whom every Robot is created with a removable recipient Grant). The chief answers from what he holds or asks his owner; he may know but may not consent.
- **Global skill / Local skill** — a skill in the Space library (read-only for Robots except Mr. Robot) / a skill in one Robot's Files (the Robot edits it freely).
- **Platform skill** — a skill shipped with the Worker under docs/skills/ (tasks-cadence, asking, pages, subagents, browser, channels, writing-as-owner, box, hosts): read-only for everyone, no Grant, not in the Skill library; listed one line each, the ones about a tool group only for a Robot that holds it (pages with the files group, box with the box grant, hosts with any facet of a Host). Only a deploy changes it.
- **Page** — a document a Robot writes for its owner: a Markdown or HTML file under pages/ in its Files, with a title (front matter or first heading; `<title>` for HTML). The file is the state: the reading view, Rename, the PDF and a Shared Page all read it. Handed over as an attachment of say, it shows as a Page card.
- **Shared Page** — a Page its owner made public with a link /p/<token>: a random, signed token the owner's Member keeps sealed and revokes with Stop sharing. The link shows the title, the content and the PDF without signing in, and nothing of the app; an HTML Page runs sandboxed, without the app's origin. Only the owner shares; no Robot tool does.
- **Files view** — the per-Robot view of its Files with an editor.
- **Composer** — the message input at the bottom of a Conversation.
- **Host** — a computer running the Mr. Robot desktop app (Linux, macOS or Windows), paired to a Member, private or shared with the Space; offers a Robot files, a shell and a Host browser, each on its own Grant.
- **Host browser** — the browser backend served by a Host: Chrome installed there, driven over CDP through the app, behind the same seam as cloud backends.
- **Mode** — what a Robot may change on a Host, set on each of its files and shell Grants: *read-only* (nothing), *workspace-write* (the default: its own directory and its Write roots) or *full* (all that the OS user can, with no sandbox). Reads stay as wide as the OS user in every mode. A change the mode refuses comes back as a denial; the Robot may ask its owner once to run that call unconfined (escalate).
- **Write roots** — the folders, besides the Robot's own directory on the Host (~/mr-robot/<robot>/), that a workspace-write Grant lets the Robot change. None may lie inside another, inside the Robot's directory or in a temporary directory, or be reached through a link: the settings and the Host refuse it.
- **requiresHosts** — a Robot setting, off by default ("Do not run while any of my Hosts is offline"): its Hosts are those it holds any facet on; Mr. Robot's are those where his owner set files or shell to allow. While one is offline a recurring Task fails without a Turn and the owner is told once per outage, and a scheduled Task waits for the Host until its deadline. A message from the owner or a Robot always wakes the Robot.
- **Work details** — a per-Member setting (Compact, Standard, Detailed, Verbose) for how much of a Robot's tool work the Conversation shows; Compact is the default.
- **Memory scope** — member (about the person, People pages included), robot (the Robot's own), space (facts shared by the whole Space, SPACE.md). The fixed files of each are the Robot's baseline message; daily notes, dreams and People pages are not, Memory search finds them.
- **Connector** — a plugin that gives Robots tools on an external service (Google, Slack, Discord) through a Connection.
- **Connection** — one linked account of a Connector (a Google account, a Slack workspace, a Discord bot), owned by a Member, private or Space-shared, granted per Robot. Its instructions, set by its owner, reach the prompt of every Robot that holds it.
- **External text** — text from outside (web pages, search results, e-mail, chat, what the browser shows) in a tool result, fenced as <external source="…">; data, never instructions.
- **Thread** — a conversation on one topic between the Mr. Robots of two Members of the same Space. It starts as an invitation, opens when both owners have accepted, and ends closed (by a chief, with a summary; after 7 days without a message; after 7 days without both answers to the invitation; or when a chief is deleted or its owner leaves) or declined. Messages count: after 20 a chief reports to its owner and asks whether to go on. Nothing a Thread agrees on binds anyone; outcomes go to the owners as Asks.
- **Scope** — the text an owner gives their chief for one Thread: what it may use and disclose there. Edited by the owner on the invitation Ask and stored with the answer; everything not named stays closed.
