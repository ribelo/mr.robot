# Glossary

Vocabulary for Mr. Robot. Specs, code and tests use these words and no synonyms.

- **Home** — one deployment's group of people who may share secrets, subscriptions, skills and robots. v1 deploys one Home.
- **Member** — a person signed in through Cloudflare Access who belongs to a Home. The first Member is the Home admin.
- **Robot** — a persistent worker owned by a Member: one Durable Object, one endless Conversation, its own Persona files, Grants and Workspace. Its work is kept as Tasks at the Home. Not a chat session; its Subagents are its tools, not Robots.
- **Subagent** — a second agent a Robot starts inside its own Durable Object for one task: a fresh context (subagent) or one that inherits the Robot's (subagent_fork), on a model from the subagents grant's list. It has the Robot's grants minus delegation, Robot messages, Threads, shared-file edits and Asks; its report wakes the Robot. Never a Robot, a Fleet row or a Task's doer. At most three run at once; depth one.
- **Mr. Robot** — the personal chief Robot every Member gets on first sign-in; it may create Robots, holds recipient Grants to every Robot the Member can reach, and creates and changes Tasks as one of them (robot_id). Sits in a side drawer of Mission Control.
- **Conversation** — the single, endless exchange with a Robot. Rendered simply as chat or fully as the Trajectory; both are the same DSH session log.
- **Trajectory** — the full session log view: Turns with their Wake-up and Task, tool calls, results, executed code.
- **Turn** — one agent-loop run of a Robot, from a Wake-up to the Robot going back to sleep. One Turn at a time per Robot; steers reach it at its next step, follow-ups wait for Turns of their own. The Robot's Subagents may still run after it ends.
- **Wake-up** — what starts a Turn or reaches a running one: a Member message, a Task whose moment came or that a person ran now, a Robot message, a Channel event, a Takeover returned, a Subagent's report, or a platform notice. Each carries a delivery, Steer or Follow-up, decided from its sender. The Turn records the Wake-up that started it as its cause.
- **Steer** — the delivery of a Wake-up from the Robot's owner (in the app or on a Channel that identifies them) or from the owner's Mr. Robot directing his staff: it reaches a running Turn at its next step, and starts a Turn when none runs. The Robot says so when it changes its work, and changes its Task focus with a reason.
- **Follow-up** — the delivery of every other Wake-up (Tasks, webhooks, other Robots, Threads, platform notices, and the owner's Ctrl+Enter): it waits for a Turn of its own. Follow-ups from one sender queued together share one Turn; a Task never shares one.
- **Task** — something to do: a title, instructions, who created it, its assignee (the doer: a Member, a Robot, or nobody; the code and tools say assignee), an optional parent Task, a Timing, and a state of open, done or cancelled. Never "in progress" or "failed": working is a fact about a Turn, failing a fact about an Attempt. Kept by the Home. Only task_done makes a Robot's Task done.
- **Timing** — when a Task wakes its doer: *unscheduled* (on the list, an optional deadline), *scheduled* (a moment, an optional deadline not before it) or *recurring* (a rule, a time zone and the next occurrence; no deadline; only a recurring Task can be paused).
- **Attempt** — one Turn of a Robot on a Task: started, finished, outcome (running, ok, failed, skipped, interrupted) and the first line of the reply. A Task keeps its last 20. Overdue is derived: a scheduled Task whose moment passed without an Attempt.
- **Mission Control** — a Member's page with two dense tables: the Fleet (each Robot he reaches: state, what woke it, the Task it works on, the running tool, since when) and the Tasks he sees, grouped by doer, parent or time. The Home admin's Whole Home switch widens both to every Robot and Task.
- **Rewind** — replacing the live session with one seeded from the log up to a chosen point; the old log goes to the Robot's archive and the Rewind can be undone.
- **Grant** — permission a Member gives a Robot: a tool, a skill, a recipient Robot, a secret. Everything is off until granted.
- **Grant proposal** — a Robot's request for additional Grants, stored exactly and answered by the owner; the stored proposal is applied, never prose.
- **Workspace** — the Robot's files in R2: Persona files, memory, notes, attachments, artefacts.
- **Persona files** — SOUL.md, IDENTITY.md, AGENTS.md, TOOLS.md, MEMORY.md and daily notes, in the Muse layout, owned by the Robot.
- **Member files** — USER.md and PROACTIVE_PREFERENCES.md, owned by a Member, mounted read-only into each of their Robots.
- **Takeover** — a Member taking control of a Robot's browser tab from their device (screen, taps, keys) and handing it back; the Robot's Turn is suspended meanwhile.
- **Live view** — watching a Robot's browser without taking it over.
- **Channel** — an adapter that turns external events into Wake-ups and Robot output into outbound messages on the same Conversation. v1: PWA and Web Push.
- **Provider** — a model source: OpenAI subscription, Anthropic subscription, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Workers AI. Credentials belong to a Member and may be shared with the Home.
- **Spend limit** — a monthly cost cap per Robot and per Member, counting every source (model, browser, computer, services); crossing it stops the Robot's Subagents, then blocks the Robot until raised.
- **Auto review** — a Robot setting: before each call of an item at allow, a reviewer model (the Robot's choice, else the Home's) classifies the call low, medium or high and allows it or denies it; a denied call becomes an Ask with the reviewer's reason, a failed review fails the call. Off by default.
- **Cache warming** — a Robot setting: while the Robot is idle, its last request is sent again for one output token at 54, 108 and 162 minutes on a one-hour cache, so the next message reads a warm cache. On for Mr. Robot by default; only for a Provider with a known cache window.
- **Idle compaction** — a Robot setting: while the Robot is idle, one compaction at 54 minutes on the warm cache, after one bounded step in which the Robot writes what matters to memory; skipped below a floor of context tokens. On for Robots other than Mr. Robot by default.
- **Computer** — a Cloudflare container a Robot with the computer grant runs commands on (Python with NumPy and Pandas, Node, Bun, git). Its disk is scratch except computer/, copied in from the Workspace at boot (with computer/setup.sh run) and back at Turn end; it stops ten minutes after its last command. Its running time is a cost.
- **Spill** — the complete result of a tool call over the inline budget, kept under spill/ in the Workspace while the model gets its head, its tail and the path; tidied after 30 days.
- **Skill library** — the Home's single collection of skills in R2, synchronised from a Git repository and extended by approved Robot proposals; granted per Robot.
- **Robot message** — a work request from one Robot to a granted recipient Robot, or its reply, delivered through a durable outbox and shown as a labelled message.
- **Code mode** — the default execution mode (DSH PTC): the model writes a program that calls its tools inside an isolate; the alternative is direct tool calls.
- **Browser backend** — what runs a Robot's Chrome behind the browser seam: Browser Run, Container Chrome, Container Chrome via VPN; later Host browser. Chosen per Robot, defaulting to the Home's.
- **Login entry** — name, username, password, websites, notes, scope; granted per Robot; filled into matching pages without the password reaching the Robot's program. Replaces Secret.
- **Ask** — a pending decision a Robot puts to its owner (grant proposal, question, setup approval, member-file edit), shown in place of the composer.
- **Global skill / Local skill** — a skill in the Home library (read-only for Robots except Mr. Robot) / a skill in one Robot's Workspace (the Robot edits it freely).
- **Files view** — the per-Robot view of its Workspace with an editor.
- **Composer** — the message input at the bottom of a Conversation.
- **Host** — a computer running the Mr. Robot desktop app, paired to a Member, private or shared with the Home; offers robots files, a shell and a Host browser on grant.
- **Host browser** — the browser backend served by a Host: Chrome installed there, driven over CDP through the app, behind the same seam as cloud backends.
- **Host tools** — host_read, host_write, host_run on a granted Host.
- **Work details** — a per-Member setting (Compact, Standard, Detailed, Verbose) for how much of a Robot's tool work the Conversation shows; Compact is the default.
- **Memory scope** — member (about the person), robot (the Robot's own), Home (shared household facts); each a set of files injected as the Robot's baseline message.
- **Connector** — a plugin that gives Robots tools on an external service (Google, Slack, Discord) through a Connection.
- **Connection** — one linked account of a Connector (a Google account, a Slack workspace, a Discord bot), owned by a Member, private or Home-shared, granted per Robot.
- **Thread** — a conversation on one topic between the Mr. Robots of two Members of the same Home. It starts as an invitation, opens when both owners have accepted, and ends closed (by a chief, with a summary; after 7 days without a message; after 7 days without both answers to the invitation; or when a chief is deleted or its owner leaves) or declined. Messages count: after 20 a chief reports to its owner and asks whether to go on. Nothing a Thread agrees on binds anyone; outcomes go to the owners as Asks.
- **Scope** — the text an owner gives their chief for one Thread: what it may use and disclose there. Edited by the owner on the invitation Ask and stored with the answer; everything not named stays closed.
